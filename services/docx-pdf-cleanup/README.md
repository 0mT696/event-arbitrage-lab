## What the $150 trial covers

**$150 fixed** for one document that meets every small-batch limit below. The price is the scope written in the supplied quote. It is not a checkout on this page, and it is not evidence that anyone has paid it.

All of the following must hold:

- One continuous manuscript, **at most 25 source pages**
- Chinese **at most 15,000 characters**, or English **at most 8,000 words** (one delivery language)
- At most 4 simple tables and 10 images kept at original size
- One existing template or written rule set

Over those limits, scanned pages that need OCR, heavy math, chart rebuilds, multi-file merges, or a bilingual layout are a separate estimate. They are not included in the $150 trial.

A qualifying trial would deliver:

1. An editable `.docx` with consistent headings, body text, lists, tables, image placement, and automatic page numbers, following a template or written rules supplied with the job.
2. A matching `.pdf` for page-by-page review. It is not a print-ready proof.
3. A page/section anomaly table: anchor, issue type, severity, excerpt, recommended action, and status. Conflicting numbers stay marked for a human check.
4. A short delivery note: input version, scope handled, open items, and what changed in the revision round.

The quoted draft includes one consolidated revision round: feedback within 3 calendar days of first delivery, limited to format misses inside the agreed template or rules. New chapters, new data, a template change, rewrites, or several rounds of feedback would be quoted again.

The quoted draft describes first delivery within 2 business days only after complete inputs, a confirmed scope, and agreed payment or escrow are all in place. Opening or downloading this sample starts none of those, and it does not start a clock.

The quote draft also mentions two payment arrangements that would have to be written down elsewhere before they could exist: marketplace escrow of the full **$150** before work starts, or a **$75 / $75** split. **Neither arrangement is available here.** This folder names no payee and has no bank account, wallet, invoice, or escrow control.

## Out of scope

- No IDML, InDesign conversion, print proof, bleed, color profile, or font-license guarantee
- No original design: no covers, illustration, infographics, or brand systems. A job would only apply a template or rules that the customer supplies and allows
- No original writing, fact audit, legal or financial advice, translation, invented citations, OCR accuracy guarantee, or authenticity endorsement
- No promise of investment results, trading results, or sales growth
- No silent rewrite of conflicting figures. In this sample, both budget totals (96,000 and 95,000) and both volunteer shift lengths (3 hours and 3.5 hours) stay on the page
- No unredacted high-sensitivity HR or finance originals, and no passwords, wallets, or keys

A designer or other designated professional still has to approve anything that would be used for a brand or publication. A technical cleanup pass does not replace that decision.
