$150 trial scope

$150 fixed for one document that meets every limit below. That figure comes from the supplied quote. It is not a button on this page, and it is not evidence that anyone has paid it.

  • One continuous manuscript, at most 25 source pages
  • Chinese at most 15,000 characters, or English at most 8,000 words, in one delivery language
  • At most 4 simple tables and 10 images kept at original size
  • One existing template or written rule set

OCR on scans, heavy math, chart rebuilds, multi-file merges, and bilingual layout are outside this price until separately estimated.

A qualifying trial would include an editable .docx, a matching review .pdf, a page/section anomaly table, a short delivery note, and one consolidated revision round for format misses inside the agreed rules. The quoted draft describes first delivery within 2 business days only after complete inputs, a confirmed scope, and agreed payment or escrow are all in place. Downloading this sample starts none of those.

The quote draft mentions marketplace escrow of the full $150, or a $75 / $75 split, only as arrangements that would have to be written down elsewhere. Neither is offered on this page. There is no payee, bank account, wallet, invoice, or escrow control here.

Out of scope

  • No IDML or InDesign file, print proof, bleed, color profile, or font-license guarantee
  • No original design, cover, illustration, infographic, or brand system
  • No original writing, fact audit, legal or financial advice, translation, or invented citations
  • No OCR accuracy guarantee and no authenticity endorsement
  • No silent fix of conflicting figures. This sample keeps both budget totals (96,000 and 95,000) and both volunteer shift lengths (3 hours and 3.5 hours)

A designer or other designated professional still has to approve brand or publication use. A technical pass does not replace that decision.

How to read the sample

  1. Skim the messy draft, then open the Word file and the PDF.
  2. Check high-severity rows in the anomaly list and confirm the conflicts were not rewritten.
  3. Read the quote file for the same limits in Chinese.
